Cyberdeck · Jun 2025

● IN PROGRESS

Cyberdeck — Car Head Unit Build

Car head unit stereo as the main screen with 2 functions: the stereo's backup camera for analog video input, and a Raspberry Pi 4 feeding video output. Raspberry Pi 5 integrated with NVMe HAT — no SD cards. Kali Linux as the OS.

Dedicated SDR-RTL and a wireless USB adapter (monitor mode + injection on 2.4/5 GHz). Button-based fan control: both switches must be on to power the 12V fan + Pi simultaneously. DIY WiFi Pineapple integration in progress (not Hak5 — custom).
