Cantenna Troubleshooting: 9 Reasons You're Getting No Signal

When cantenna troubleshooting no signal stops working, the right move is rarely the first guess. This is a real-world fault-tree based on what the KGTHETECH lab has actually seen break — not theory.

Always check power first

Half of every troubleshooting session ends at the power supply. Voltage drop under load is invisible until it isn't. Measure with a real meter, not the chip's self-report.

Connections, then config

Physical layer before logical layer. Reseat connectors. Inspect cables. Only then start poking at config files. Software-first troubleshooting eats hours.

Reproduce the failure on purpose

Intermittent issues don't fix themselves. If you can't reproduce, you can't fix. Build a 30-second cycle that exposes the failure consistently, then start removing variables.

When to walk away

Eight hours in, fresh eyes beat fresh techniques. Sleep on it. The next morning you'll spot the missing ground in 90 seconds.
